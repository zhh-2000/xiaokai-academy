On the morning of August 31, 2026, local time, Chinese President Xi Jinping arrived by car at the Harmony Palace in Bishkek to attend the welcome ceremony held by Kyrgyz President Sadyr Japarov. This is an important part of President Xi's itinerary for the 2026 Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) Summit.

When meeting with President Japarov, Xi said that China is making every effort to search for Chinese and foreign nationals who are missing in the mudslide disaster in China's Xizang Autonomous Region. Japarov expressed his sympathies and solidarity with China over the mudslide disaster, demonstrating the friendship between the two countries.

The SCO Summit brings together leaders from China, Russia, Kyrgyzstan, and other member states to strengthen the Shanghai Spirit of mutual trust, mutual benefit, equality, and respect for diverse civilizations. China Dispatches Y-20 Transport Planes with 50 Tons of Relief Supplies to Nepal

On August 30, 2026, China delivered 50 metric tons of emergency relief supplies to Nepal and dispatched nine tunnel rescue experts to assist with rescue operations following a deadly mudslide on August 26. Two Y-20 transport aircraft operated by the People's Liberation Army Air Force carrying 13,200 relief supplies arrived at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu on Sunday morning.

The supplies included tents, blankets, sleeping bags, compressed food items, and first-aid kits. They will be distributed in disaster-hit areas to help meet the basic living and emergency medical needs of those affected. Four Chinese tunnel rescue experts and their equipment arrived in Kathmandu with the supplies, while five others had already reached the disaster area by helicopter from Gyirong County in China's Xizang Autonomous Region on Saturday.

The delivery of relief supplies and the expert missions marked the latest progress in implementing China's assistance package for Nepal, which Foreign Minister Wang Yi said during a phone call with his Nepali counterpart Shisir Khanal included emergency cash assistance and humanitarian aid. Wang said a sudden glacier collapse in the China-Nepal border area had triggered severe mudslides, causing significant casualties and leaving people missing on both sides.

Wang described it as the most serious cross-border disaster between the two countries in recent years, adding that the complexity and difficulty of the rescue and relief operations were unprecedented. A meeting of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee had called for emergency aid to Nepal's disaster-hit areas and international rescue cooperation, noting that it was an important political decision reflecting China's friendship with Nepal.

Source: China Daily, Xinhua, 2026-08-31
