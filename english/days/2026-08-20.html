Passage 2: Tsinghua Students Volunteer at FAST Telescope Site

On August 19, 2026, China Daily reported that 20-year-old Liu Chang, a student from Tsinghua University's Department of Engineering Physics, volunteered at a primary school in Kedu Town, Pingtang County, Guizhou Province. Pingtang County is home to China's FAST (Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical Telescope), the world's largest radio telescope. The volunteers designed courses that combined scientific exploration, sports, artistic expression, and life education. Students learned to build telescope models and water rocket models. The volunteers also used sports activities to help rural girls build "body confidence." This two-week summer camp demonstrates a new model of combining university volunteer service with local science culture and children's education in remote areas.
