Passage 1: Lin-gang Special Area Eyes 7.5% Growth

Lin-gang Special Area, part of the Shanghai Pilot Free Trade Zone, is aiming for average annual GDP growth of 7.5% during the 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-30), with over 6% of GDP directed to R&D. Officials expect 70 nationally pioneering institutional innovation cases. The integrated circuit industry target is 120 billion yuan, while the intelligent automobile industry target is 300 billion yuan by 2030. High-end equipment manufacturing is expected to exceed 100 billion yuan by 2030. Tang Hao, executive deputy director of Lingang's administrative committee, Tao Changsheng, deputy director of Shanghai municipal financial services office, and Chen Hao, deputy director of Shanghai Municipal Commission of Commerce, outlined these goals at a briefing on Friday, August 22, 2026.

Passage 2: China Pledges Greater Fiscal Support in H2

Vice-Minister of Finance Liao Min announced at a news conference on Friday, August 22, 2026, that China pledges greater fiscal support in the second half of the year. China's general public budget expenditure reached 16.29 trillion yuan ($2.4 trillion) in the first seven months, up 1.3% year-on-year. Revenue increased 5.8% to 14.37 trillion yuan. By the end of July, local governments had issued 2.4 trillion yuan in new special-purpose bonds. A 6-policy fiscal-financial coordination package, backed by 100 billion yuan, benefited residents on 113 million occasions and 6.22 million enterprises. The consumer goods trade-in program allocated 187.5 billion yuan, generating 1.32 trillion yuan in sales. China's full-year growth target stands at 4.5 to 5 percent.
