Passage 1: China New Energy Vehicles Cross 50 Percent Cumulative Sales Milestone

China's new energy vehicles have crossed a 50 percent cumulative sales milestone, marking a historic turning point for the country's automotive industry. According to data released on August 23, 2026, NEVs now account for more than half of all cumulative car sales in China. This achievement reflects years of government policy support, including subsidies, tax exemptions, and infrastructure investment. China has been the world's largest NEV market for nine consecutive years. In the first seven months of 2026, NEV sales reached 4.82 million units, up 32.5 percent year-on-year. Battery electric vehicles accounted for 62 percent of NEV sales, while plug-in hybrids made up 28 percent, and fuel cell vehicles accounted for the remaining 10 percent. Major Chinese automakers including BYD, Geely, and NIO have expanded production capacity to meet growing demand. The milestone also reflects China's progress toward its carbon neutrality goal by 2060.

Passage 2: China's Foreign Trade Shows Two-Way Balance, APEC Customs Activities Conclude in Dalian

China's foreign trade maintained a balanced two-way flow in the first seven months of 2026, with imports growing by 22 percent and exports growing by 14 percent. The activities included the APEC Customs Joint Action Plan and the signing of multiple agreements to facilitate global trade. The events focused on supply chain connectivity, digital customs transformation, and fighting counterfeiting. Participants from 21 APEC economies discussed ways to speed up customs clearance and reduce trade costs.
