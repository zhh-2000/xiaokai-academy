APEC Role in AI Cooperation Draws Praise

APEC is playing an important role in translating artificial intelligence cooperation from strategic consensus into tangible action, experts said, as they laud China for providing significant public goods to foster regional AI development.

As AI reshapes the global economy at breakneck speed, the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation, the region's most influential economic cooperation mechanism, is rapidly advancing its agenda for AI collaboration. A significant step was taken at the 2025 APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Gyeongju, South Korea, where the APEC Artificial Intelligence Initiative (2026-2030) was adopted. This initiative, the first APEC leaders' outcome focused specifically on AI, sets clear directives to promote secure, accessible, and reliable AI ecosystems for all.

Since taking over as the APEC host last November, China has prioritized digital and smart transformations as new growth drivers for APEC economies. In July, at the APEC Digital and AI Ministerial Meeting held in Chengdu, member economies adopted the Chengdu Statement. This key document focuses on promoting universal and meaningful digital connectivity and leveraging digital technologies and AI for economic growth.

At the APEC High-Level Forum on AI, also held in Chengdu in July, China announced plans to establish a center in Qianhai, Shenzhen, to promote APEC cooperation on cross-border data flows. The planned center will serve as an open platform for policy research, regulatory alignment and services for businesses. Kang Hogu, director of the Sino-Korea Economy/Society Institute, called the move strategically significant for balancing development and security in the Asia-Pacific.

Nvidia to Acquire Hugging Face for $12.93 Billion

US chipmaker Nvidia has agreed to acquire artificial intelligence developer platform Hugging Face for about $12.93 billion, Nvidia founder and CEO Jensen Huang announced on September 4, 2026.

Huang said in a company blog post that the two companies will work together to scale Hugging Face's platform, strengthen its infrastructure and expand access to AI for developers and institutions worldwide. Hugging Face will remain an open platform for the entire AI ecosystem, Huang said. "NVIDIA compute will not be required to build on or deploy through Hugging Face," Huang noted.

He added that Hugging Face will continue to support open-source and open-weight models from different model developers, as well as multi-cloud and multi-accelerator development and deployment. According to Huang, more than 18 million developers, researchers and creators use Hugging Face, where they share more than 3 million models, 500,000 datasets and 1 million applications.

More than 200,000 companies use the platform to discover, evaluate, customize and deploy AI. Nvidia has also been expanding its involvement in open AI models. Huang said the company has released more than 500 models and more than 250 open datasets on Hugging Face, describing Nvidia as the platform's largest contributor of open models and data. Hugging Face was founded by Clement Delangue, Julien Chaumond and Thomas Wolf.
