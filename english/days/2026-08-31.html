Xizang Mudslide Disaster: 16 Dead, 546 Missing, Massive Rescue Operation Underway

On August 30, 2026, the government of the Xizang Autonomous Region held a press conference in Gyirong Town to provide updates on the "8·26" mudslide disaster that struck the Gyirong border port. As of 6:00 p.m. on August 29, the disaster had claimed 16 lives, with 546 people still missing. Among the missing, 261 are foreign nationals from 23 countries, including Nepal (104), India (49), the United States (18), and Latvia (33).

The disaster originated on the Nepal side of the China-Nepal border. At approximately 10:52 a.m. Beijing time on August 26, a glacier broke off at an altitude of about 5,200 meters on the northern slope of the Langtang Lirung peak in Nepal. The ice-rock avalanche fell rapidly from 5,200 meters to about 4,000 meters, scouring the mountainside and forming a massive mudslide that traveled approximately 22 kilometers along the Purungpu Tsangpo and Donglin Tsangpo rivers, reaching the Gyirong port at an altitude of about 1,800 meters. The entire event took only about 6 to 7 minutes.

The mudslide leveled approximately 0.7 square kilometers of land, destroying 27 buildings and their associated facilities. Roads, communications, and power lines leading to the Gyirong port were completely severed. A barrier lake formed at the port, with a dam averaging 60 meters in height, 1,100 meters in length, and 300 meters in width. By August 28, the barrier lake had begun overflowing, and as of August 30, it had essentially drained.

The rescue operation has mobilized 2,141 personnel, including the People's Liberation Army, armed police, China Fire and Rescue, border defense, public security, and China Anneng. A total of 269 rescue vehicles and 3,922 pieces of equipment have been deployed. The rescue forces are advancing into the core disaster area via "water, land, and air" routes. The Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Emergency Management allocated 120 million yuan, while the National Development and Reform Commission arranged 100 million yuan to support rescue and post-disaster recovery.
