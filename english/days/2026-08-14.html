Passage 1: C919 Completes First International Commercial Flight

China's domestically developed C919 passenger jet has completed its first international commercial flight, marking a significant milestone in the country's aviation industry. The aircraft, carrying 111 passengers, flew from Beijing to Ulaanbaatar, the capital of Mongolia, on August 12, 2026. This marks the first international route opened by the C919 since its commercial operation began. Upon arrival, the aircraft received a traditional "water cannon salute" at Chinggis Khaan International Airport, symbolizing the historic achievement. The successful international flight demonstrates that China's self-developed trunk airliner has officially entered a new phase of regularized international operations.

Passage 2: China's New Energy Vehicle Sales Hit New High

China's automobile exports reached 1.043 million units in July 2026, marking the second consecutive month that exports have exceeded 1 million units, according to data released by the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers on August 12. More notably, sales of new energy vehicles (NEVs) accounted for 60.4% of total new vehicle sales in July, the first time that NEV market share has exceeded 60%. This milestone demonstrates the rapid transformation of China's automotive industry toward electrification. The strong performance reflects growing consumer acceptance of electric vehicles and government support for green transportation. Industry experts predict that the trend will continue as battery technology improves and charging infrastructure expands across the country.
